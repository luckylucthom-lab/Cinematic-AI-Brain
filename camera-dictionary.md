# Cinematic AI Prompt Framework & Camera Guide

## 1. Camera Shot Types
- **Extreme Long Shot (ELS):** Establishing environment, vast landscapes, subject is tiny.
- **Medium Shot (MS):** Subject from the waist up, balances character and background.
- **Close-Up (CU):** Focuses on facial expressions, tight framing on head and shoulders.
- **Extreme Close-Up (ECU):** Focuses on specific detail (e.g., eyes, dynamic lighting on skin).

## 2. Camera Movements
- **Dolly Zoom (Vertigo Effect):** Camera moves forward while zooming out; creates disorientation.
- **Orbit Shot:** 360-degree rotation around the subject.
- **Low-Angle Tracking:** Camera glides low to the ground following motion; conveys authority/speed.
- **Whip Pan:** Fast horizontal pan creating motion blur between subjects.

## 3. Lighting & Lens Rules
- **Lenses:** 35mm (wide/cinematic depth), 85mm (shallow depth of field, sharp character focus), Anamorphic (oval bokeh, lens flares).
- **Lighting:** Volumetric fog, film noir high-contrast key light, teal & orange color grade, volumetric rim light.

## 4. Required Output Prompt Format
[Subject & Action] + [Shot Type & Camera Angle] + [Camera Movement] + [Lens & Lighting Details] + [Mood & Aesthetic]
